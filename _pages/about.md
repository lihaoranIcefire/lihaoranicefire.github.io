My research topic was [Polylogarithms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polylogarithm) and their applications within number theory.

I currectly work as a fixed-income quantitative researcher at LSEG. I enjoy learning about all of natural sciences, I also love hiking and traveling. If you have great ideas/places to share, please feel free to reach me by email.
