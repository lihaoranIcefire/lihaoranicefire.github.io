# Godel's first incomplete theorem

Consider formula $\varphi(x)$:

> if $x$ is the Godel number of a formula with one free variable, then the sentence formed by plugging its Godel number into itself is not provable in $T$

Suppose the Godel number of $\varphi(x)$ is $d$, then the so-called Godel sentence $\varphi(d)$ implies $\varphi(d)$ is not provable in $T$, therefore it is a sentence that is true yet unprovable!!!
